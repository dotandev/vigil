Vigil
Source

A watchman that is paid by what it guards, and dies when you do.

Vigil holds an estate at an address that has no private key: NEAR's MPC network signs for it, and only when this contract asks. An agent watches for your check-ins, paying rent every second from its endowment. If you go silent, it settles the estate to the heirs you named. If it runs out of money first, it dies and can never touch the estate.
